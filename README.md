# carburetor-aircleaner-remote-filter-or-snorkle-stuff-
CARFSS Yet another 3D part i need for a classic car 

Basically to fix some Airflow around the engine of a 1972 bronco the Stock air cleaner was removed and replaced with a 14 inch open element under hood filter. This has proved to be a problem in that the Carb is sucking in hot under hood air and the Holley Sniper EFI no likey since the MAF temp and thus the electronics and throttle body is getting quite toasty. To fix this i'm making a remote air filter that will pull in fresh cold air from the outside and keep that airflow around and over the top of the engine that i need. Ford broncos.... the over heating champions
